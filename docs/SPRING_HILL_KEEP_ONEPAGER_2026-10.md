# Spring Hill, FL — Keep market check

*STACK internal · 2026-10-19 · from the Keep market calibration (`docs/KEEP_MARKET_CALIBRATION_2026-10.md`)*

## Verdict: PASS at $290/SF. It works only as a cheap, fast LuxeLocker-style campus — and only if all-in cost lands well under our $235/SF break-even.

| | Spring Hill | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| **Supported price** | **~$120–190/SF** (≈ $210 with a strong product) | No comparable supports $290. Our plan's break-even is **$235/SF**. |
| **Sales-speed risk** | **57 min** to the nearest wealthy area | Same range as our slowest comp: Woodlands TX, 64 min, 57 of 99 units still unsold after ~3 years. |
| **Wealth nearby (20 min)** | 2,317 households earning $200K+; **0.5%** of homes worth $1M+; typical home ~$196K | The thinnest wealth of any site we measured. |
| **Works buyer (15 min)** | 8,701 self-employed filers; ~330 small contractor/repair businesses; Hernando contractor count **+63%** since 2017 | Real demand for ~800 SF contractor bays. This is the site's strength. |
| **Seasonal homes** | 7% | Moderate snowbird presence (Lake Havasu, a fast seller, is 18%). |
| **ParcelLogic lane** | **Growth corridor**, with the far-from-wealth flag | Not counted as a garage-condo needle. |

Spring Hill sits with LuxeLocker's cheaper markets, and on $1M+ home share it is below all of them. After adjusting for
local home prices, those campuses sold at 0.46–0.89× the local home $/SF; at Hernando's ~$173/SF that is about
**$80–155/SF**. The band above stretches to $190–210 only with a finished clubhouse product.

## What would have to be true to build it

1. **All-in cost (land + build + soft) well under ~$160/SF**, so a $180–200 blended price still clears a margin.
   Cheap land and approved civils help, but our standard product doesn't get there.
2. **Contractor-heavy mix:** mostly ~800 SF works bays, few premium units. The works buyer is the deep pool here.
3. **Presales before going vertical** (our 40% / 10% deposit rule). With Woodlands-type distance from wealth, don't rely
   on post-completion absorption.

If those three can't be met, pass. A site such as Westampton (~$255/SF supported) or Robbinsville (~$270) fits our product.

## Caveats
- The pricing model is directional: 26 campuses in four operator families; treat bands as ±15%.
- No garage-condo campus exists in the Spring Hill area to test directly, and Tampa's north suburbs are about an
  hour away. The ~$210 upper case assumes some of that buyer pool makes the drive.
